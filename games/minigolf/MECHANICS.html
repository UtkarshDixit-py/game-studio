Putt Duel mechanics

Every rule and number in the game, taken from the code and checked against the running physics. Distances are in course units (u). One unit is one pixel of the 400 × 560 court.

The match

Match play against a bot over three holes. Each hole is worth one point. Most points after hole 3 wins. Equal points is a draw.

You are out of a hole when your shot count reaches the cap and the ball is not in the cup. A star raises your cap by one for that hole.

A turn

  • Players alternate, one shot each. If the other player has already finished the hole, you keep shooting.
  • On your first turn you tap inside the start zone to place the ball, then shoot. Placing and the first shot are one turn.
  • A turn ends when the ball stops, drops into the cup, or has rolled for 12 seconds.
  • Both balls stay on the court. They never collide.

Phases in the match state: placing → aiming → rolling → holeOver → matchOver.

Aiming

  • Touch anywhere, drag, release. The ball goes the opposite way to the drag, like a slingshot.
  • Power is drag length divided by 180 px, capped at 100%. Releasing below 5% cancels the shot.
  • Launch speed is power × 640 u/s. The aim line is power × 120 px long.

Roll is the straight-line distance with nothing in the way, and time to stop is on grass. The court is 400 × 560 u, so a full-power shot always reaches a wall. Distance is speed² ÷ 480 on grass and speed² ÷ 2400 on sand.

Hazards

Wall
Solid. The ball bounces off with 85% of its speed into the wall. The court edges behave the same way.
Sand
Slows the ball at 1200 u/s², five times grass. A full-power shot stops within 171 u.
Water
The ball goes back to where that shot started and stops. The shot still counts.
Conveyor
The belt moves at 160 u/s in the arrow direction. Friction pulls the ball toward belt speed, so a ball on a belt never rests. It rides at 160 u/s until it leaves. Going against a belt costs speed at the same rate as grass, but a ball that stalls is carried back.
Portal
Radius 14 u. A ball that enters comes out 23 u in front of the paired portal at the same speed, heading in that portal's fixed exit direction whatever the entry angle. It cannot re-enter a portal until it has rolled clear.
Breakable prop
The first hit bounces the ball with 50% of its speed into the prop. The prop is then gone for both players until the hole ends.
Star
Radius 12 u. Rolling over it gives that player one extra shot on this hole. Once taken it is gone for both players.
Cup
Radius 11 u. The ball drops when its centre is inside the cup and it is moving at 260 u/s or less, which is 41% of full power. A faster ball rolls straight over.

Order of checks in each step: friction, move, walls and props, portal, water, star, cup, roll limit.

The bot

The bot plays by the same physics as you. It has no hidden information and no speed or accuracy bonus. It picks a shot by trying many and keeping the best.

Shots tried per turn14436 directions, one every 10°, at four powers: 30%, 55%, 80% and 100%. Each is rolled to rest with the real physics.
RankingHoling out beats everything. Otherwise the shot that stops closest to the cup wins. Any shot that touches water ranks below every dry shot.
Placement3 spotsCentre, left-middle and right-middle of the start zone. It runs the full search from each and takes the best, 432 simulated shots.
Aim error±3.6°Random nudge added to the chosen direction.
Power error±3.75 ptsRandom nudge added to the chosen power, kept between 5% and 100%.
Pacing0.6 s + 0.5 sIt waits, shows its aim line, then shoots.
  • It looks one shot ahead only. It does not plan two-shot routes.
  • The error sizes come from a skill setting of 0.75. At skill 1 there is no error. At skill 0 it is ±14.3° and ±15 points.
  • The error uses a seeded random number stored in the match state. The same seed and the same player inputs replay the same match. Add ?seed=N to the URL to set it.

Saving

The game stores wins, losses, draws, the mute setting and the match in progress in the browser under minigolf:save. It saves after every finished turn and at the end of each hole, never while a ball is rolling. The title screen shows the record and a Continue button when a match is in progress.

Not in the game

Online play, two players on one phone, a turn timer, ball-to-ball collisions, more than three holes, camera movement, and anything involving real money.
